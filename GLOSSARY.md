# Real Denim Fit

Finds raw denim jeans worth looking into. It works out what a person wants at each point from their body and the fit they're after, and compares that against the measurements brands publish for every size.

## Language

### What brands make

**Brand**:
A company that makes raw denim jeans, such as Naked & Famous, Iron Heart or Nudie.
_Avoid_: maker, label, manufacturer

**Cut**:
A brand's pattern for a pair of jeans, such as the Weird Guy, 634S or Lean Dean, made in a range of sizes and often in many fabrics.
_Avoid_: fit, model, style

**Line**:
Whether a brand files a cut as men's, women's or unisex.
_Avoid_: gender, category, department

**Product**:
One cut in one fabric: the thing someone buys, with its own sizes, list price and shrinkage.
_Avoid_: item, SKU, jean

**Size**:
One size of a product, with the garment measurements the brand publishes for it. The same cut can measure differently in different fabrics.
_Avoid_: tag size

**Garment Measurements**:
Measurements taken on a pair of jeans, as opposed to on a person.
_Avoid_: specs, size chart

**List Price**:
What a brand asks for a product on its own store, in one currency, on the date it was seen.
_Avoid_: MSRP, retail price, sale price

**Stretch**:
Describes a product whose fabric contains elastane. A product without any is **rigid**.
_Avoid_: comfort, flex

**Sanforized**:
Describes denim that was pre-shrunk at the mill, so it shrinks only a little in its first wash. Denim that wasn't is **unsanforized**.
_Avoid_: pre-shrunk, loomstate, shrink-to-fit

**Finish**:
How a product's denim looks when it's sold: **raw** (never washed), **rinsed** (washed once, not faded) or **washed** (faded on purpose at the factory).
_Avoid_: wash, treatment, colourway

**Fabric Weight**:
How heavy a product's denim is, in ounces per square yard, such as 14 oz or 21 oz.
_Avoid_: weight class, heft

### Measuring

**Measuring Point**:
The exact spot where a measurement is taken, such as the knee 13″ below the crotch. Brands use different ones, and the user measures at all of them.
_Avoid_: method, position

**As-Sold Measurements**:
Garment measurements of a product as the brand ships it.
_Avoid_: raw measurements, pre-wash

**After-Wash Measurements**:
Garment measurements of a product as it will be after its first wash. Matches are judged on these.
_Avoid_: shrunk measurements, post-soak, washed measurements

**Shrinkage**:
How much a product gets smaller in its first wash, as its brand states it.
_Avoid_: shrink

**Tolerance**:
How far a brand says its published measurements may be off, such as ±½″.
_Avoid_: margin, accuracy

### Measurements

**Waist**:
The distance around the top of the waistband.

**Front Rise**:
From the crotch seam to the top of the waistband, at the front.

**Back Rise**:
From the crotch seam to the top of the waistband, at the back.
_Avoid_: rear rise

**Seat**:
Across the widest part of the seat, below the waistband.
_Avoid_: hip

**Thigh**:
Across the top of the leg, at or just below the crotch.
_Avoid_: upper thigh

**Knee**:
Across the leg partway down, at a point that depends on the brand.

**Leg Opening**:
Across the bottom of the leg.
_Avoid_: hem, ankle, opening

**Inseam**:
From the crotch seam to the bottom of the leg, along the inside.
_Avoid_: length, leg length

### What the user brings

**Body Measurements**:
Measurements taken around the user's body with a soft tape. Every user gives these.
_Avoid_: body size, sizing

**Desired Fit**:
How the user wants their jeans to sit, chosen as a room, a leg shape and a rise rather than measured.
_Avoid_: style, preference, fit type

**Room**:
The part of a desired fit that says how much ease the user wants through the seat and thigh: snug, regular or roomy.
_Avoid_: looseness, slimness

**Leg Shape**:
The part of a desired fit that says how the leg runs below the knee: tapered, straight or wide.
_Avoid_: taper, leg style

**Rise**:
The part of a desired fit that says how high the waistband sits: low, mid or high. The measurements are always **front rise** and **back rise**.
_Avoid_: rise height, waist height

**Reference Pair**:
A pair of jeans whose fit the user knows, either measured flat by them or picked from the catalogue. Adding one is optional and sharpens their matches.
_Avoid_: favourite pair, benchmark, my jeans

**Feel**:
How a reference pair is on the user at one measurement: too tight, just right or too loose (too short or too long for inseam and rise).
_Avoid_: rating, feedback, review

**Nudge**:
A small shift the user asks for at one measurement, a little more or a little less, on top of the target.
_Avoid_: adjustment, offset, tweak

**Try-on Brand**:
A brand the user has marked as one they can try on locally.
_Avoid_: local brand, nearby brand, stockist

### Working out what fits

**Ease**:
How much bigger than the body a pair is at one measuring point.
_Avoid_: room (the choice), allowance, wearing ease

**Ease Table**:
The researched ease each desired fit calls for at each measuring point, with a source on every number.
_Avoid_: fit table, room table

**Minimum Ease**:
The least ease a pair needs at a point to be wearable, such as being able to sit down. Stretch products need less.
_Avoid_: floor, limit

**Target**:
The range a garment measurement should fall in at one measuring point, with a middle that's ideal: around the reference pair's reading where it has one, otherwise the body plus the ease the desired fit calls for.
_Avoid_: goal, ideal

### What the app gives back

**Match**:
A product in one size, with how close each of its garment measurements comes to the target.
_Avoid_: recommendation, result, suggestion

**Closeness**:
How near a measurement comes to the middle of its target, as a percentage. A match's closeness is that of its furthest-off measurement.
_Avoid_: score, match rate, rating
