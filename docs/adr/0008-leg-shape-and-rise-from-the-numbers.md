# Leg shape and rise come from the numbers, not the brands' labels

Brands use names like "slim straight" and "medium rise" inconsistently, so the app ignores them and works both out from each size's own measurements.

- **Leg shape** comes from knee minus leg opening, measured flat. Tapered is 3 cm or more, straight is 1 to 3 cm, and wide is under 1 cm. That sorts the Weird Guy as tapered, and the 634S and CS-105x as straight. Brands take the knee at different heights, so this is approximate.
- **Rise** has no standard anywhere, and raw denim runs higher than mall brands. So a size's rise is set by where its front rise falls among every product in the catalogue at the same waist: the lowest third is low, the middle third is mid, and the top third is high.

## Consequences

- A product's rise can change from mid to high when brands are added to the catalogue, without anything about the product changing.
