# Matches are judged after the first wash, without stretch from wear

A Reference Pair has almost always been washed, so every product is compared as it will be after its first wash. That means the brand's after-wash chart when it has one, the chart as it is when the product is sold already washed, and otherwise the chart adjusted by the brand's stated shrinkage. Products that will shrink by an amount the brand doesn't give are marked "shrinkage unknown" and ranked after the rest.

The waist stretching as it's worn is deliberately left out of the numbers. Brands give only rough figures (about ½–1″), and building a guess in would make every rigid product look smaller than its chart says. It's shown as a note on rigid products instead.
