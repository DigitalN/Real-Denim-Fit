An independent check of the catalogue on branch `claude/build-v1-c9bd5f` (commit `8214cd8`): six brands, 718 products. Nothing under `catalogue/` was changed. Every brand page was re-fetched on 2026-11-21.

The two sets of "left out on purpose" numbers are all on the brand's own `EXTRACTING.md` list of brand typos. Not one recorded number differs from its source. List price, `sanforized` and `finish` matched on every product checked: 111 in all, which is the 30 Rogue Territory products plus the 81 sampled from the other brands.

There's one problem, and it comes from Nudie's own data, not from the extraction. Nudie's size widget gives **Slim Jim Dry Two** exactly the measurements of a different cut, Regular Alf, in all 65 sizes. The catalogue copied them faithfully, but they can't be right for a slim straight cut. See fix 1.

## How it was checked

- **Rogue Territory** charts are pictures, so they got a blind second read:
  - I read every number on every chart myself, enlarged 2.5×, and wrote all 2,058 down before opening the catalogue's files.
  - Apple's built-in text recognition (Vision) then read every image six times, at different sizes and contrasts, as a third read that never saw the catalogue.
  - The three reads were compared cell by cell by a script.
- **The other five brands** were sampled with a fixed seed (`20261121-<brand>`), so the draw can be repeated:
  - The draw takes one random product from every cut (only 9 of Nudie's 17 cuts, to stay at 10%), then more at random until at least 10% of the brand is covered.
  - Iron Heart and Momotaro had no `raw` product (Iron Heart) or only `raw` products (Momotaro) in the random draw. So I added one random product per missing `finish` (1 for Iron Heart, 3 for Momotaro) to test those paths too. They're counted in the totals above.
  - Every published number of every sampled size was compared with the live page, along with `sanforized`, `finish` and the list price.
- **The scripts aren't vacuous:** I planted one wrong number per brand in a throwaway copy of the catalogue, and each brand's check flagged it.
- **The scripts and raw reads** are in the session notes folder, under `verify-catalogue/`, outside the repo. The repo has only this report. The files there:
  - `myread.txt`: my Rogue Territory read
  - `nudie-widget.txt`: the Nudie widget reads
  - `sample.json`: the drawn sample
  - `scripts/`: the scripts, including the Vision OCR tool

## Rogue Territory: the blind second read

**Coverage:** all 30 products and all 294 sizes, 7 measurements each, so 2,058 cells.
- The 30 products use 27 different pictures. The Stanton 11oz and the Strider 11oz each share one image between Indigo and Stealth, and the two washed Stantons use byte-identical uploads. That makes 1,855 distinct printed cells.
- I downloaded each image from the URL in `catalogue/rogue-territory/EXTRACTING.md`.
- The live product pages still link those same 27 images (30 of 30 products), and all 30 list prices match the catalogue.
- **`sanforized` and `finish`:** 30 of 30 match the page wording.
  - The 8 pages that don't say "sanforized" are exactly the 8 that `EXTRACTING.md` lists as taking the FAQ default (`true`). No page says unsanforized.
  - River Wash, Vintage Wash and Stealth Wash are `washed`, and the "pre-rinsed" Strider is `rinsed`.
  - The 24 `raw` pages mention no garment wash, only care advice.

**Results:**
- **Catalogue vs my read: 2,058 of 2,058 agree.**
  - That includes the one cell the catalogue leaves out: the `standard-issue-15oz-stealth` size 28 waist, printed as "5". I read "5" too, and so did four OCR passes. It stays out.
- **OCR vs my read:**
  - The passes agreed on a value for 2,040 cells, and every one matched me. In 8 of those cells, one pass disagreed (for example "19" for "11") and was outvoted by the others.
  - OCR never contradicted me.
  - It skipped 17 cells (14 distinct ones), all a lone "8", which the recogniser tends to drop. Re-run on a close crop of each cell, it read "8" in 10. I looked at the last 4 enlarged 8×, and all are plainly "8".
- The 480 px rendition of the Stealth Strong Taper chart, read by OCR, agrees with the 2,626 px original on all 76 of the cells OCR read.

**Disagreements:** none, so there was nothing to give a third read. Every number in the catalogue is backed by two independent reads, plus a machine read that agrees.

**One caveat about the "blind" part:** I read `EXTRACTING.md` before reading the images, so I had seen about a dozen numbers in advance. Those are the "5" waist, the Stanton 11oz knees at 29 and 30, the Stealth Strong Taper front rises at 35 to 38, and the washed Striders' waists at 36 to 40. The OCR, which saw nothing in advance, agrees with all of them.

**Judgement: trust as is.** Under the brief's rule (keep a number only where two reads agree), all 2,057 recorded numbers stay, and the "5" stays out. The oddities `EXTRACTING.md` already notes are printed that way, and both reads confirm them. Examples are the Stanton 11oz knee of 7.5 at size 29, and the Stealth Strong Taper front rise jumping to 12.25 at size 36. They're the brand's own numbers.

## Limits of this check

- For brands other than Rogue Territory, this is a sample. It shows the extraction method is faithful, but it can't rule out a slip in a product nobody drew.
  - For Iron Heart, Naked & Famous and 3sixteen, the same scripts could check every product in a few minutes, if you want full coverage.
  - Nudie is the slow one: 27 of its 5,278 sizes were read off the widget.
- `sanforized` defaults that come from a brand's FAQ were checked as rules: the page says unsanforized → `false`, otherwise `true`. The FAQ pages themselves weren't re-read.
- Prices change. These were checked on 2026-11-21.

## Fixes

1. **`catalogue/nudie/slim-jim.json`: remove the product `slim-jim-dry-two`.**
   - Its only chart is Regular Alf's: all 65 sizes are identical to `regular-alf-dry-selvage`.
   - In `catalogue/nudie/EXTRACTING.md`, add it under "Dry, but no measurements (left out)", with the reason "Virtusize serves Regular Alf's measurements for this product". Check it again on the next run.

That's the only data fix. **Rogue Territory needs none:** two reads agree on every number, so all 2,057 recorded numbers stay, and the size-28 "5" waist stays out.

Two optional ways to keep this automatic, rather than one-off:

- **A catalogue validation that fails when two different cuts carry an identical chart.** It would have caught the Slim Jim case, and it passes on everything else today.
- **For image charts (Rogue Territory), a second read by the OCR script on every `/update-catalogue` run.** It's in the notes folder, under `verify-catalogue/scripts/` (`ocr.swift`, `ocr_passes.sh`, `rt_compare.py`). Any cell where the OCR passes agree with each other but not with the catalogue would be flagged for a look.
